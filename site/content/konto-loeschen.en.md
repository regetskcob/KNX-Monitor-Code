<p class="t-kennung hz-kennung">KNX Monitor · Account</p>

KNX Monitor does not offer accounts at the moment. You need none to use it, and data on your devices (project, recordings, settings) stays there.

**Did you have an account?** Send an email with the subject “Delete account” from the address you signed in with to the address in the [legal notice](https://www.regetskcob.de/legal/). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm it.
