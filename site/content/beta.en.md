The apps are built and currently in beta. Anyone can join, and every feature is free, including those of Plus and Pro. To test you need a KNX IP interface or IP router; without an installation you can try everything in the demo house.

## iPhone, iPad and Mac

1. Install Apple's [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) app.
2. Open the [beta link](https://testflight.apple.com/join/fsM3ufJK) on the device and tap "Accept".
3. Tap "Install" in TestFlight. New versions arrive there automatically.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or later; on the Mac, macOS 13.5 or later.

## Android

1. [Join the Google group](https://groups.google.com/g/knx-monitor-beta) with your Google account. Only then does Google Play let you into the test.
2. [Join the test](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/de.kalkar_it.knxmonitor) and choose "Become a tester".
3. Install Busclear from Google Play through the link on the test page. Updates arrive through Google Play as usual.

Requires Android 6 or later. It sometimes takes a few minutes until Google Play knows you joined.

## The thank-you

**90 days of Pro, free.** When Busclear launches on the App Store or Google Play, the thank-you starts on the first launch of the store version: 90 days of Pro on the device you used the beta on. You don't have to do or sign up for anything; afterwards the app carries on with Free until you choose Plus or Pro yourself. The app shows you until when it applies.

## Feedback

We welcome bugs and wishes: through TestFlight feedback (take a screenshot and share it), in the [GitHub issues](https://github.com/regetskcob/KNX-Monitor/issues) or to [support@busclear.de](mailto:support@busclear.de). If it is about your installation, the [diagnostics package](/en/feedback/) helps.
