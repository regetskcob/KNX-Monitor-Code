need:
  - "**A KNX IP interface or IP router** in the same network, for example from MDT, Gira, Weinzierl or ABB. Almost every installation has one."
  - "**Your ETS project** (`.knxproj`), if you have it. It works without: you teach addresses in by pressing push buttons."
  - "**No risk for the installation:** Busclear listens. It only writes when you explicitly ask it to."

terms_title: "KNX in four terms"
terms:
  - term: "Device"
    text: "A push button, actuator, detector or weather station. Each has an address of its own like 1.1.12 by which it is recognised."
  - term: "Group address"
    text: "A function such as \"switch kitchen light\", written as 1/2/3. Whoever belongs to this address listens and reacts."
  - term: "Telegram"
    text: "A short message on the bus: who sends, to which group address, with which value. Busclear shows each one in plain text."
  - term: "ETS project"
    text: "The file in which your electrician planned everything. It provides the names. Ask for it, it belongs to your installation."
try_title: "How to start without any risk"
try:
  - title: "Look at the demo house"
    text: "No installation and no interface needed: \"Try the demo without a KNX system\" on the start page shows a sample house with real sequences."
  - title: "Learn KNX"
    text: "Five short lessons with a quiz, from \"How KNX switches\" to \"Questions and answers\". \"Try it\" takes you to the matching place in the app."
  - title: "Teach your installation"
    text: "No project at hand? Press a push button, the app catches the address, and you give it a name. Room by room this becomes your own directory."
ask_title: "Questions you can answer afterwards"
ask:
  - "What happens when I press this push button, and which actuator reacts?"
  - "Why did the light come on at night, and who switched it?"
  - "Which devices never report, and which send unusually often?"
  - "Is the installation healthy before I call the electrician?"
first_title: "The first steps"
first:
  - title: "Connect"
    text: "Busclear looks for the interface in the network. If it reports \"no free tunnels\", another program is holding it, often ETS or a visualisation."
  - title: "Load the project"
    text: "Import the ETS project, also with a password. Addresses like 1/2/3 become names, rooms and devices. Without a project: press a push button and name the address."
  - title: "Read the overview"
    text: "Values sorted by topic: lighting, heating, energy, alarms. Tap a card to see its history and who sent."
  - title: "Check the health"
    text: "One run queries the status addresses and names what does not answer or sends unusually often. It is the quickest look at the state of the installation."
  - title: "Keep watching"
    text: "With the add-on or the box the recording goes on. Watchers tell you when an address reaches a value, and the \"Last 7 days\" card sums up the week."
